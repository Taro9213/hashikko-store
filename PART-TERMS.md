We do not rewrite your part (apart from the preview pieces in section 2). If something needs to change, we ask you in a note, and you share an updated version.

You can ask us at any time to take your part out of the store by emailing hashikkotaro@gmail.com. We will remove it from the store. People who already added it can keep using the copy they have, and the permission in section 2 continues for those copies.

## 7. What we may do

We may decline a part, or remove it at any time, as described in the [Guidelines](GUIDELINES.md). Parts removed for safety reasons are also turned off on computers that already added them. We may change or stop the store.

## 8. No payment

Sharing a part is free, and no one is paid for it. If paid parts are ever offered, they will have separate terms, and you will be asked before any of your parts are sold.

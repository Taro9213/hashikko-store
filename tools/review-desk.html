Review desk

Everything people send from Hashikko arrives here. Pick something on the left.

  1. Submissions: read the automatic checks, try the part, tick the four review points, then decide.
  2. Reports: parts people flagged. Pause a part while you look, take it down, or close the report.
  3. Appeals: creators who think a decision was a mistake. Look again and answer.
  4. Channels: every creator and their record (warnings and strikes).

How rule-breaks work (the same ladder YouTube uses): the first rule-break is a warning. After that, each one is a strike: strike 1 stops sharing for 1 week, strike 2 for 2 weeks, and a 3rd strike within 90 days removes the channel and all its parts. Warnings and strikes go away after 90 days. Severe cases (scams, fake logins, malware, sexual content involving minors) remove the channel right away.
